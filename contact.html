Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you go

When is the best time to visit Siargao?

Weather and sea conditions change throughout the year. Check a current local forecast and ask operators about conditions for your intended activities.

Can I visit Magpupungko Rock Pools any time?

The tidal pools are best seen around low tide and may be covered by the sea at high tide. Check the local tide schedule and follow safety guidance before visiting.

Are the listed prices final?

No. They are realistic sample estimates for a classroom project, not confirmed operator quotes. Fees, meals, boat costs, transport, and seasonal rates should be confirmed before booking.

Does this website confirm a reservation?

No. The booking form prepares an email inquiry only. A booking is confirmed only after an operator replies and confirms availability and terms.

What should I bring?

Bring drinking water, sun protection, swimwear, a reusable bag, cash for local fees, and suitable footwear. Follow local guidance and leave no litter.
